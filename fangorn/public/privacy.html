Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Cookies

We do not set cookies. We use no advertising, retargeting, or cross-site tracking of any kind, and we do not sell or share personal data with third parties for those purposes.

If you sign in, our authentication provider (Privy) stores session data in your browser's local storage so you stay logged in. Clearing your browser storage signs you out.

Account data

If you sign up through Privy, we collect and store:

  • the email address you sign up with, and
  • your wallet address (whether you connect an existing wallet or Privy creates one for you).

We use these solely to identify your account, associate it with your data and usage, and contact you about the service. Authentication is handled by Privy — see the Privy privacy policy. If you never sign up, we collect none of this.

Note that on-chain activity — including your wallet address and any transactions you make — is public by nature and permanently recorded on the blockchain. We cannot delete or modify it.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate traffic (page views, referrers, country, general device type). It is cookieless, does not fingerprint visitors, and does not track you across sites. See Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Logs

Our hosting and API infrastructure keeps standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, request path, user agent) for security and debugging. These are retained for a limited period and not used to build profiles.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data at any time by emailing us. Deleting your account removes your email and wallet association from our systems; it cannot remove data already published on-chain or to a public network.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and, for signed-up users, notify you by email.
